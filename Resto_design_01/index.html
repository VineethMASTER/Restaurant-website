Our Story

Founded in 2010, The Gilded Fork brings farm-to-table freshness with a touch of urban elegance. Our chefs specialize in seasonal ingredients sourced from local artisans.

Our Passionate Staff

Our team is the heart of the experience. From our world-class sommeliers to our dedicated floor staff, we ensure every guest feels like royalty.

Fine Hospitality

We believe dining is more than just food; it's a memory. We focus on every detail, from the ambient lighting to the perfect table setting.

The Culinary Masters

Led by Chef Julian Vane, our kitchen combines traditional French techniques with modern molecular gastronomy to surprise your palate.
